### AI Copilot Orchestrator

Its manifest-backed feature ID is `CrestApps.OrchardCore.AI.Chat.Copilot`, and it is categorized as Artificial Intelligence - Orchestrators. It depends on `CrestApps.OrchardCore.AI`, so Orchard Core enables that dependency when this feature is enabled.
